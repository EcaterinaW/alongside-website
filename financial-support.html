FINANCIAL SUPPORT

Resources that may help ease the financial burden.

Childhood cancer can create financial pressure for the whole family. Alongside brings together organizations and support resources from different parts of the world that may help with treatment-related expenses and everyday needs.

A note about financial assistance

Eligibility requirements, available funding, application procedures, and the types of support offered can change. Some organizations may require families to apply through a hospital, social worker, or healthcare team. Always check the organization's official website for the most current information.
