FINANCIAL SUPPORT

Resources that may help ease the financial burden.

A childhood cancer diagnosis can create unexpected financial stress for families. The organizations below offer resources that may help with housing, food, transportation, travel, and other expenses.

$

EVERYDAY EXPENSES

Family Reach

Offers financial assistance for eligible non-medical expenses, including rent or mortgage payments, utilities, groceries, and transportation.

Visit Website →

HOUSING · FOOD · TRANSPORTATION

Pinky Swear Foundation

Provides financial support to eligible families of children with cancer for everyday expenses such as housing, food, gas, utilities, and transportation.

Visit Website →

FAMILY ASSISTANCE

The B+ Foundation

Helps eligible families with expenses related to a child's cancer diagnosis, including rent, mortgage payments, travel costs, and some prescription expenses.

Visit Website →

TRAVEL FOR TREATMENT

Alex's Lemonade Stand Foundation

The Travel For Care program may provide eligible families with assistance for transportation and lodging when a child must travel for specialized cancer treatment.

CancerCare

LODGING · FAMILY SUPPORT

Ronald McDonald House Charities

Helps families stay close to children receiving medical care by providing accommodations, meals, and other support through local Ronald McDonald House programs.

Before applying

Eligibility requirements, available funding, and application procedures can change. Some programs require an application through a hospital social worker or another member of the child's healthcare team. Please visit each organization's official website for current information.
