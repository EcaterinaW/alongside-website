ALONGSIDE

About This Website

Alongside is an informational platform created to support parents and guardians who are facing the difficult experience of receiving a diagnosis for their child.

Why Alongside?

Receiving a diagnosis can be overwhelming. Parents may suddenly have to navigate unfamiliar medical information, financial difficulties, emotional stress, and many important decisions. In such moments, finding reliable information and knowing where to ask for help can make the situation a little less overwhelming.

Alongside was created to bring useful and accessible support resources together in one place, so that families do not have to search for everything on their own.

What Can You Find Here?

The website focuses on two important areas of support:

  • Financial Support — information about verified charitable organizations, ways to request financial assistance, and official platforms where families can organize fundraising.
  • Psychological Support — information about free psychological services, peer-support opportunities, self-help practices, and useful resources for parents experiencing stress, anxiety, or emotional exhaustion.

Our Purpose

Our purpose is simple: to help families feel less alone and make reliable support easier to find. Alongside does not provide medical diagnoses or replace professional medical or psychological care. Instead, it serves as a starting point for discovering resources and support that may be available to families.

Because no family should have to face a difficult journey alone.
