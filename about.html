ALONGSIDE

A place to find support when you need it most.

Alongside is an informational platform created for parents and guardians facing the difficult experience of a childhood cancer diagnosis. Our goal is to make reliable support resources easier to find and help families feel less alone.

♡

Why Alongside?

A childhood cancer diagnosis can bring many challenges at once. Families may have to navigate unfamiliar information, unexpected financial difficulties, emotional stress, and difficult decisions. Finding trustworthy resources can be overwhelming, especially during an already difficult time.

Alongside was created to bring important support information together in one place, so families can spend less time searching and more time focusing on what matters most.

→

How to use this website

Alongside brings together practical and emotional support resources in two main areas. Visit the Financial Support page to explore organizations and platforms that may help families with financial challenges. The Psychological Support page provides information about counseling, support groups, coping resources, and emotional support for children and their families.

You can explore the resources that are most relevant to your situation and visit the official websites of the organizations listed to learn more about their services and eligibility requirements.

i

Who is Alongside for?

This website is designed primarily for parents and guardians of children who have been diagnosed with cancer. It may also be useful for family members, caregivers, and anyone looking for reliable information about support resources for a child and their family.

!

An informational resource

Alongside does not provide medical diagnoses, treatment, or professional psychological care. The information on this website is intended to help families discover available support and resources. Eligibility requirements and available services can change, so please check each organization's official website for the most current information.
